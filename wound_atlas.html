Evidence & scope

This atlas uses a five-step teaching framework: hemostasis, inflammation, tissue formation, surface closure/contraction, and remodeling. Standard physiology groups tissue formation and epithelialization within the same proliferative phase, so there are four overlapping phases biologically—not five rigid, consecutive phases. Timelines are illustrative and vary by wound, location, species, and health status.
